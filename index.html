Straight answers.

What exactly do I get to send someone?

A link to a working chatbot for their business, and a page showing what a month of unanswered enquiries is worth to them with every assumption on screen. The bot knows their services, hours, phone and rating because it was built from their Google listing. It has no prices, so it declines to quote rather than guess — which is the thing that makes it safe to send to a stranger.

Where does the leak number come from?

Their review count bands the size, their trade sets how many people enquire per job and how many of those close, and what we find on their site sets the share that never reach anyone. It is a model and the X-Ray page shows every step — change any input and watch it move. It is split in two on purpose: what a bot recovers, and what is lost before anyone could answer. Only the first number goes in a bot pitch.

Does it pitch businesses that already have a chat widget?

No. It detects about forty chat and messaging platforms and marks those businesses "skip" — their message becomes a warning instead of a pitch. In the run on this page that was 37 of 275. Pitching an answering bot to someone who already has one is the fastest way to look like a mailshot.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You pick a city and a niche. Everything else runs on a schedule and lands as a list on your phone every Monday. Hosting the bots is one file you paste into a free Cloudflare account, once.

Does it send the messages for me?

Deliberately not. It writes every message and queues it, and you press send. Automated outbound at volume burns sending domains and gets social accounts restricted — and an automated claim that turns out wrong is a deal you cannot recover. Ninety seconds of your morning is what keeps the channel alive.

Can I still sell websites?

Yes, and the X-Ray tells you who actually needs one — it separates enquiries nobody answered from enquiries that died before anything could. The second number is the website conversation, and it only comes up when it is real.
